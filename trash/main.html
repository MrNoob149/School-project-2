Swinburne Vietnam Alliance Program

Swinburne Vietnam Alliance Program (Swinburne Vietnam) is an international alliance between Swinburne University of Technology in Australia and FPT University in Vietnam.

Swinburne University of Technology is a globally recognised university with a history of more than 100 years. FPT University is one of Vietnam’s leading universities, with internationally recognised programs and accreditations.

Students in Vietnam can access high-quality programs and learn from the strengths of both universities.
